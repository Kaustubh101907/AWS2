### ☁️ What is this Service?

This is the actual **Creation Phase** of a Virtual Private Cloud (VPC). You have two choices when building your network on AWS: you can use the "magic wizard" (VPC and more) to automatically build the entire network structure instantly, or you can build it bare-bones (VPC only) and wire all the complex routers and subnets together manually.

---

### 📝 The Breakdown

Here is everything mentioned in your transcript, simplified into one easy-to-understand paragraph:

When creating a VPC in AWS, the console offers an automated wizard that allows you to fill in the blanks with your CIDR blocks (`172.20.0.0/16`), Subnets, and Availability Zones, and AWS will automatically stitch the entire infrastructure together for you, complete with a visual flowchart of the traffic routes. However, to truly understand how networking works under the hood, the instructor cancels out of this wizard and selects the "VPC only" option, which simply draws the massive `/16` boundary without creating any subnets or gateways. During the setup, you must ensure the "Tenancy" setting is left on "Default" (shared hardware), because selecting "Dedicated" hardware will result in an incredibly expensive bill! Similarly, NAT Gateways carry a hefty hourly charge, which is why the instructor explicitly limits the design to just one NAT Gateway to save costs during the tutorial, rather than the recommended two for high availability. The wizard also introduces the concept of VPC Endpoints, which act as a secret tunnel allowing private subnets to securely access other AWS services (like S3) directly, completely bypassing the public internet and NAT Gateways. Finally, once the "VPC only" is created, AWS silently generates a default route table for it, but the instructor plans to ignore it and build custom ones from scratch in the next steps.

### 🚀 Summary

* **Two Ways to Build:** AWS has an automated "VPC and more" wizard, but using "VPC only" forces you to learn how to manually connect the networking components yourself.
* **The Cost Warning:** **NAT Gateways are expensive.** Only spin them up when necessary, and remember to delete them when you are done practicing!
* **Tenancy:** Always select **Default** (Shared Hardware). "Dedicated" hardware is for enterprise clients with strict compliance needs and huge budgets.
* **VPC Endpoints (The Secret Tunnel):** A way for your highly secure Private Subnets to access AWS services (like S3 buckets) directly without ever touching the public internet or using a NAT Gateway.
* **Next Steps:** The "VPC only" creation is just the empty shell; the next step is manually carving out the four `/24` subnets inside of it.
